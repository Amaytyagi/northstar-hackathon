The real problem

Most people don’t fail because they lack money. They fail because they lack systems.

Moving to a new city is exciting, but it also brings high rent, emotional stress, social pressure, and temptation to overspend. Many young people buy expensive things to “fit in” before they understand real costs.

💸

Spending pressure

People buy flashy items to impress others, even when their budget doesn’t support it.

📍

New city confusion

They don’t know real local costs, which leads to poor decisions and stress early on.

🧠

No life plan

There is no simple system that explains how to survive the first 90 days away from home.

Our solution

NorthStar is a personal city-life coach for first-time movers.

We combine budgeting, city onboarding, and behavior guidance into one simple app that helps users make better decisions before they overspend or panic.

📊

Smart budget builder

Suggests realistic budgets based on income, rent, food, commute, and personal spending.

🛡️

Impulse risk detector

Flags “show-off spending” patterns and warns users before they make expensive mistakes.

🧭

90-day starter roadmap

Guides users through safety, savings, setup, and lifestyle habits in a new city.
