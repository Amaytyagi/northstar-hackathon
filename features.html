NorthStar Features

Built for the people who are moving into a new life.

NorthStar is more than a budget tracker. It brings structure, clarity, and emotional confidence to the most stressful time in a young adult’s life: moving to a new place without a safety net.

💸

Smart Budget Building

Set realistic monthly budgets with automatic guidance based on rent, food, travel, and lifestyle spending.

🛡️

Impulse Risk Detection

Catch the “show-off” spending traps before they turn into debt, stress, or regret.

📍

City Cost Awareness

Understand the real cost of living in a new city so users don’t get surprised by normal monthly expenses.

🧠

Behavior Guidance

Help people make calmer, smarter decisions when social pressure and emotional stress are high.

✅

90-Day Starter Plan

Give users a structured first 90 days with clear goals around savings, stable spending, and adjusting to the city.

📈

Life Stability Insights

Track preparation, savings, and emotional confidence so the first months feel manageable, not overwhelming.
